Smart Classroom Attendance System

---

## Target Users

The Smart Classroom Attendance System is designed for:

- Students who need a simple way to submit attendance
- Lecturers who need to monitor attendance records
- Administrators who need to manage users and review attendance activity

---

## Headline

# Simple, Fast, and Reliable Classroom Attendance

---

## Supporting Message

Record attendance using Student ID and Password, view attendance history, and monitor attendance performance through role-based dashboards.

---

## Problem Statement

Traditional classroom attendance methods can take too much time and may lead to missing, duplicated, or inaccurate records. Students may also find it difficult to check their attendance history, while lecturers and administrators may not have a clear summary of attendance activity.

---

## Solution

The Smart Classroom Attendance System provides a simple web-based platform where students can log in, submit attendance, and view their records. Lecturers can review attendance information, while administrators can manage users, update attendance status, and monitor system metrics.

---

## Key Features

1. Student ID and Password login
2. Role-based dashboards
3. Attendance recording
4. Attendance history
5. Search and filtering
6. Dashboard metrics
7. Attendance status management

---

## Main Value Proposition

The system helps schools reduce manual attendance work, improve record accuracy, and provide clearer attendance information to students, lecturers, and administrators.
