# Legal and Ethical Checklist

## Data Collection
- Student ID: Necessary (used for login and identification)
- Password: Necessary (authentication)
- Attendance record: Necessary (core system function)

## Privacy Consideration
- No sensitive personal data (e.g. phone, address) is collected
- Only required academic data is used

## Ethical Considerations
- No misleading claims in the system
- Students cannot mark attendance for others
- Admin actions are clearly defined and limited

## Compliance
- Data collected is minimal and relevant
- No unnecessary or excessive data stored

## Conclusion
The system follows basic legal and ethical requirements for an MVP prototype.
