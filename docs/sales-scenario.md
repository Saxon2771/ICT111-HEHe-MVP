# Sales Scenario

## Project Name

**Smart Classroom Attendance System**

---

# Overview

The **Smart Classroom Attendance System** is a web-based application designed to simplify classroom attendance management. Instead of using paper attendance sheets or manual roll calls, students log in with their **Student ID and Password** to record their attendance, while lecturers can monitor attendance records through a digital dashboard.

---

# Customer Scenario

**Customer:** University Lecturer

**Organization:** University Faculty

**Current Situation:**

At the beginning of every class, lecturers spend several minutes calling students' names and marking attendance manually. This process is slow, interrupts teaching time, and increases the risk of recording errors. Students who arrive late or forget to sign the attendance sheet may also be marked incorrectly.

---

# Customer Problems

The lecturer currently experiences the following problems:

- Manual attendance takes too much class time.
- Attendance records may contain errors or missing entries.
- Students sometimes forget to sign the attendance sheet.
- Attendance reports must be prepared manually.
- It is difficult to monitor attendance history over the semester.

---

# Proposed Solution

The **Smart Classroom Attendance System** provides a simple digital solution where:

- Students log in using their **Student ID and Password**.
- Students record their attendance with one click.
- Attendance is stored automatically.
- Lecturers can immediately view attendance records.
- Attendance history and reports are available from the dashboard.

---

# Customer Benefits

After implementing the system, lecturers and students receive several benefits:

### For Students

- Faster attendance recording.
- No paper attendance sheets.
- Easy access to attendance history.
- Simple login using Student ID and Password.

### For Lecturers

- Reduced attendance-taking time.
- More accurate attendance records.
- Easy attendance monitoring.
- Automatic attendance reports.

### For University

- Digital attendance management.
- Reduced paperwork.
- Better record organization.
- Easier future system expansion.

---

# Demonstration Scenario

The following scenario will be used during the project demonstration.

### Step 1 – Open the System

The lecturer opens the Smart Classroom Attendance System before class begins.

### Step 2 – Student Login

Students enter their Student ID and Password.

### Step 3 – Authentication

The system verifies the login information.

### Step 4 – Record Attendance

Students click the **Record Attendance** button.

### Step 5 – Attendance Confirmation

The system displays a confirmation message indicating that attendance has been successfully recorded.

### Step 6 – Lecturer Dashboard

The lecturer logs into the dashboard and views the updated attendance list.

### Step 7 – Attendance Report

The lecturer reviews attendance statistics and student attendance history.
