# User Personas

## Project Name
Smart Classroom Attendance System

---

# Persona 1: Harry

## Basic Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Name | Harry |
| Age | 24 |
| Year | Second-Year Student |
| Department | Information Technology |
| Technology Level | Intermediate |
| Devices Used | Android Smartphone, Laptop |

### Background

Harry is a second-year student who attends multiple classes every week. He uses his smartphone for learning, checking schedules, and communicating with classmates.

### Goals

- Record attendance quickly.
- Avoid attendance mistakes.
- Spend less time waiting before class.

### Frustrations

- Forgets to sign paper attendance sheets.
- Long attendance queues.
- Attendance records are sometimes incorrect.

### Needs

- Fast QR-code attendance.
- Instant attendance confirmation.
- Attendance history.

### Quote

> "Attendance should only take a few seconds."

# Common Pain Points

- Manual attendance takes too much time.
- Attendance records may contain errors.
- Students sometimes forget to sign attendance sheets.
- Paper attendance sheets can be lost or damaged.

# Shared Goals

- Faster attendance process.
- Accurate attendance records.
- Mobile-friendly QR-code attendance.
- Less classroom disruption.

# Evidence from Lab 03

These personas were developed from customer discovery findings collected during Lab 03. Students consistently reported that manual attendance is time-consuming, prone to errors, and inconvenient. Most respondents expressed interest in using a QR-code-based attendance system.
