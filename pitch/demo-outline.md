# Demo Outline for Final Pitch

## 1. Opening Problem

Manual attendance can take several minutes of class time and may create missing, duplicate, or inaccurate records. Students also cannot easily confirm their attendance history, while lecturers and administrators must spend extra time organizing records.

## 2. Target User

- University students
- Lecturers and course instructors
- Academic administrators
- Department coordinators

## 3. Product Solution

The Smart Classroom Attendance System is a browser-based prototype that provides:

- Student ID and Password login
- Role-based dashboards
- Attendance submission
- Automatic attendance storage
- Student attendance history
- Lecturer record viewing
- Search and filtering
- Status updates
- Dashboard metrics
- Planned report export and complete admin management

## 4. Value Proposition

The system helps educational institutions reduce manual attendance work, improve record accuracy, and give students and staff faster access to attendance information.

## 5. Demo Flow

1. Introduce the manual attendance problem.
2. Open the landing page.
3. Click the demo or login button.
4. Log in as a student.
5. Submit attendance.
6. Show confirmation and attendance history.
7. Log in as a lecturer.
8. View, search, and filter records.
9. Update a status and show dashboard metrics.
10. Show the current administrator interface.
11. Explain FR8, FR9, and FR10 final improvements.
12. Show user testing evidence.
13. Close with product value and next action.

## 6. Evidence

### Customer Discovery Evidence

Previous student and lecturer feedback identified:

- Slow manual attendance
- Missing or inaccurate records
- Difficulty checking attendance history
- Extra lecturer administration work

### Validation and Testing Evidence

- Lab 8 task success rate: 80%
- Lab 8 average feedback score: 4.1
- Lab 11 core prototype tests: Main workflow passed
- Lab 13 simulated full completion rate: 62.5%
- Lab 13 full or partial completion rate: 82.5%
- Critical remaining requirements: FR8, FR9, and FR10 implementation gaps

### Product Metrics

The prototype can display:

- Total users
- Total students
- Total attendance records
- Present records
- Absent records
- Attendance rate
- Active students
- Most active course

## 7. Business and Sales Message

The initial pilot can be offered to one class or department at no cost. Direct classroom demonstrations, lecturer referrals, university email, and class group chats can attract early users. Future pricing could use a department subscription or university license.

## 8. Closing Value Message

Attendify replaces slow manual attendance with a clearer digital workflow. Students can submit and review attendance, lecturers can find and manage records, and administrators gain better visibility. The final release will complete the remaining report and administrator requirements before Lab 14.
